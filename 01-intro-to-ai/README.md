# 01 — Introduction to Artificial Intelligence


## What this chapter covers

A broad first look at AI before diving into Machine Learning specifically:

- What AI is, and how it differs from human intelligence
- A timeline of AI history: the Dartmouth Conference (1956), the AI "winters,"
  Deep Blue, AlphaGo, and the rise of LLMs like ChatGPT
- Why AI is exploding *now* — the convergence of data, compute, and algorithms
- The relationship between AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, and
  Computer Vision (AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL)
- Data Mining vs Machine Learning — different goals, shared techniques
- A first pass at the 3 types of learning (supervised, unsupervised,
  reinforcement) — expanded on in later chapters
- Real-world applications by sector (healthcare, finance, transportation, etc.)
- Current technical and ethical challenges: bias, interpretability, privacy
- Where AI is heading: generative AI, multimodal models, AGI

This sets up the vocabulary and mental model used throughout the rest of the
series.
